CRR - Coefficient of Rolling Resistance. You can usually find this value for your specific tyre with a web search. Note that the surface you are riding affects this value signicantly as well.

CdA - Coefficient of Aerodynamic Drag. A slippery road bike and rider can get to around 0.30. A full aerodynamic setup for Remco Evenepoel gives him 0.16!
